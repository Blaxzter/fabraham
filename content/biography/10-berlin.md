## TU Berlin — B.Sc.

Computer science at TU Berlin, finished at 1.8 — and the degree was the smaller
half of it. At **GT-ARC** I worked on multi-agent systems; as a student developer
at **RWTH Aachen** I helped build the identity-management apps behind
~**47,000 students and 9,000 staff**. Systems that size are unforgiving about the
unglamorous parts: migrations, permissions, and the Monday morning after a
release.
