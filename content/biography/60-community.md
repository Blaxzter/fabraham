::bio-row{when="since 2020" kind="volunteer" name="Kirchentagswoche" role="programme, live stream, Mediathek"}
The site for the church's Kirchentag week, built end to end: the programme
people plan their days with, the live stream, and the Mediathek with the
recordings. Rebuilt for 2026 on Nuxt 4 and Cloudflare Workers.
::

::bio-row{when="since 2024" kind="volunteer" name="The new hymnal" href="https://github.com/Blaxzter/gb-dashboard" role="editor, pipeline, app"}
The church is putting together a new Gesangbuch, and I build the software
around it: an editor where texts meet melodies and open tasks get tracked,
scripts that prepare the sheet music, and the
[offline app](https://github.com/johkirche/gb-app-pwa) the congregation sings
from. Used every Sunday, so bugs get reported fast.
::

::bio-row{kind="volunteer" name="And around it" href="https://www.johannische-kirche.org" role="smaller sites, services, advice"}
The smaller websites and services the church needs along the way, and a hand
in the technical planning when a new project starts.
::
