## Maastricht University

M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence, graduated with 8.25 out of 10. The programme is
built around team projects, and I focused on generative models. My
[master's thesis](https://github.com/Blaxzter/Master-Thesis_GAN-level-gen) used
GANs to generate stable structures for Angry Birds levels, and the results became
a [paper at AIIDE 2023](https://github.com/Blaxzter/Utilizing-Generative-Adversarial-Networks-for-Stable-Structure-Generation-in-Angry-Birds).

::bio-work{when="2020–2022"}
Aachen is just across the border, so I kept a part-time job at
[**RWTH Aachen**](https://www.rwth-aachen.de) throughout: identity-management
apps used by about 47,000 students and 9,000 staff, where I worked on
permissions, data exports and a process for archiving and deleting user data.
In the winter of 2021 I also did an internship there, building an
outlier-detection system that flags suspicious activity in file-server backups.
::
